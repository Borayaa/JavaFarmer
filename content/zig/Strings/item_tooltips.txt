@item_descr_fertilizer = Call `_ = farm.useItem(.Fertilizer);` to instantly remove 2s from the plants remaining grow time.
@item_descr_weird_substance = Call `_ = farm.useItem(.Weird_Substance);` on a bush to grow a maze, or on other plants to toggle their infection status. Obtained from infected plants. Fertilizing plants infects them.
@item_descr_water = Used to water the ground by calling `_ = farm.useItem(.Water);`.
